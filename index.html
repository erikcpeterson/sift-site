Your inbox, sifted on your own server.

Sift is a self-hosted email triage tool. It connects to the mail accounts you already have, learns what deserves your attention, and gets the rest out of the way.

What Sift does

  • Syncs your mail over IMAP. Gmail and Google Workspace sign in with Google OAuth; every other provider uses a normal IMAP login. All accounts land in one place.
  • Triages instead of filing. Each message is scored as needs you, worth a glance, or noise, using rules you teach it and a small language model that runs on hardware you control.
  • Acts on your behalf when you ask. Archive, mark read, flag, move, delete, unsubscribe and reply, from a Mac or iPhone client that talks only to your own server.
  • Stays private. There is no Sift cloud. Your mail is cached on the machine you run the daemon on and nowhere else.

How Sift uses Google data

When you connect a Google account, Sift requests the Gmail IMAP and SMTP permission (https://mail.google.com/). It uses that permission to read the messages in the mailbox folders it syncs, to change their state (archive, flag, read/unread, move, delete) when you take an action, and to send messages you compose. That data is used only to show you your mail and to triage it. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to outside AI services.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Who runs it

Sift is written and maintained by Erik Peterson. Each installation is operated by the person whose mail it processes; there is no shared service.
